About Us & History

Why Was This Foundation Established?

The Pillars of Knowledge & Preaching

Allah has blessed different people with various talents and opportunities to serve His religion. Some become scholars, Imams, or preachers who lead prayers, deliver sermons, and guide the community. Through this work, they earn immense rewards from Allah and help build society. We respect them deeply, as they are the pillars of knowledge and leadership in Islam.

How Can Everyone Else Serve Islam?

Not everyone is given the opportunity by Allah to become a scholar or a preacher. Many young people possess a deep love for the religion but lack formal scholarly knowledge. If scholars serve Islam through knowledge, how can everyone else serve Islam and earn rewards? The answer is: By serving the community. Supporting orphans, feeding the hungry, and helping the poor are all monumental deeds.

In the Footsteps of Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA)

The foundation is named after the second Caliph of Islam, a man of profound justice, compassion, and care for the weak. He personally looked after orphans and the poor at night. Those who cannot deliver a sermon can support an orphan. Those who cannot teach knowledge can help pay for school fees. Everyone has something to offer: knowledge, wealth, time, or skills.

🌍 Our Vision

To be a leading center that provides complete care for orphans and beautifully spreads the teachings of Islam, inspiring a united community built on faith, kindness, and social justice.

🌟 Our Mission

To serve Allah by empowering vulnerable orphans through education and life development, while strengthening Islamic values by bringing people together to do good deeds.
